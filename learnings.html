OSI 7-layer model · what I learnt

It's not about memorising the layers

When I first learnt the OSI Model, I thought it was mainly about memorising the seven layers.

After understanding it better, I realised the important part is knowing how data changes as it moves through a network.

ApplicationData
PresentationData
SessionData
TransportSegments
NetworkPackets
Data LinkFrames
PhysicalBits
Sending: encapsulation →← Receiving: decapsulation

At the Application, Presentation and Session layers, it is called data. At the Transport layer, it becomes segments. At the Network layer, it becomes packets. At the Data Link layer, it becomes frames, and at the Physical layer, it is transmitted as bits.

This process is called encapsulation. Each layer adds the information needed to help deliver the data correctly. On the receiving side, the process is reversed through decapsulation.

Learning this helped me understand networking and troubleshooting more clearly, so I created this infographic to make the concept easier to remember.
